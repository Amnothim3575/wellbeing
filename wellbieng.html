Our Core Philosophy

Three Pillars of Digital Well-being

Digital well-being isn’t about abandoning modern technology—it’s about designing friction for distractions and flow for what matters most.

1. Conscious Boundaries

Replace reactive scrolling with intentional check-ins. By curating notifications and creating tech-free zones, you regain control over your daily rhythm.

2. Deep Single-Tasking

Constant context-switching drains cognitive energy. Structured focus intervals paired with mindful breathing allow your brain to enter restorative flow states.

3. Restorative Unplugging

Protect your circadian rhythm and nervous system with evening screen curfews and analog weekend rituals that reconnect you with nature and community.

Practical Digital Boundary Strategies

Attention Protection

The 10-Minute Notification Audit

Turn off all non-human notifications. Keep alerts exclusively for real people (calls, direct messages) and silence promotional badges, news flashes, and social likes.

Pro Tip: Enable "Scheduled Summary" so non-urgent updates arrive twice a day on your terms.
Dopamine Reset

Switch to Grayscale Display

Vibrant red badges and high-contrast app icons are engineered to hijack visual attention. Removing color makes your phone feel like a calm tool rather than a slot machine.

Pro Tip: Set a triple-click accessibility shortcut on iOS or Android to toggle grayscale after 8 PM.
Circadian Care

60-Minute Sunset Screen Curfew

Blue light and stimulating feeds suppress melatonin production. Charge your phone outside the bedroom and swap late-night scrolling for paper reading or stretching.

Pro Tip: Use a classic analog alarm clock so your phone never needs to sit on your nightstand.
